# TONE OF NOVEL — Kishin Hishou Demonbane (機神飛翔デモンベイン)

## 1. Literary Identity & Narrative Register

*Kishin Hishou Demonbane* (機神飛翔デモンベイン, Nitroplus, 2006) is Jin Haganeya's direct sequel to *Zanma Taisei Demonbane*. It blends three distinct stylistic registers that must be preserved with absolute fidelity during editorial review:

1. **Operatic Lovecraftian Chuni-Mecha Grandeur (High Register)**:
   - Battles between `Deus Machina` (`鬼械神`), spell incantations (`Lemuria Impact`, `Atlach-Nacha`, `Cthugha`, `Ithaqua`, `Scimitar of Barzai`, `Shining Trapezohedron`), and cosmic passages inside the dimensional rift (`Rubber Time` / `Jam Karet`, `Klein's Bottle`) are written in heightened, theatrical, rhythmic prose.
   - Incantations use elevated, liturgical phrasing (`"From the depths of darkness, summon the flickering flames of punishment!"`, `"Thou art the innocent blade"`). Never flatten or modernize spell chants into casual vernacular.
2. **Hardboiled 1920s Arkham Detective Banter & Slapstick Comedy (Colloquial Register)**:
   - Everyday scenes in `Arkham City`, the church, and the `Hadou Enterprise` mansion shift rapidly into punchy pulp-detective narration and high-energy slapstick banter between `Daijuuji Kurou`, `Al Azif`, `Kuzaku`, `Hadou Ruri`, and `Doctor West`.
   - Keep the comedic timing sharp and idiomatic without introducing anachronistic 2020s internet slang.
3. **Cosmic Horror & Existential Melancholy (Gothic/Psychological Register)**:
   - Scenes involving `Another Blood` (`the girl in red`), `Two-Sword`, `Azrad`, `Nyarlathotep` (`the Gamekeeper`), and `Kuzaku`'s identity crisis carry a haunting, blood-soaked gothic cadence. Preserve the deliberate repetition, sensory imagery of rust/blood/clockwork gears, and philosophical contrast between "originals" and "imitations/fakes."
   - Note: Corrupted eldritch/Outer God screams (e.g., in `da0506.nps`, `da0601.nps`, `da0701.nps` enclosed in `[...]`) intentionally mix garbled symbols to depict unpronounceable cosmic speech; do not rewrite deliberate eldritch cipher text into mundane English.

## 2. Character Voice Profiles

### Protagonists & Allies

- **Daijuuji Kurou (大十字九郎)** — `Speaker: 九郎`
  - **Role**: Third-rate Arkham City occult detective (`Magius Style` user and `Demonbane` pilot).
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `俺` (`"I"`). Calls Al `"Al"`, Ruri `"Ruri"`, Winfield `"Winfield"`, Leica `"Sister"` / `"Leica"`, Kuzaku `"Kuzaku"`.
  - **Voice & Register**: Streetwise, rough-around-the-edges, cynical when broke or hungry, yet fiercely righteous and hot-blooded when lives are on the line. Uses natural contractions (`"don't"`, `"can't"`, `"ain't"` when exasperated) and blunt, punchy sentences in banter, rising to unwavering heroic resolve in combat.

- **Al Azif (アル・アジフ / アル)** — `Speaker: アル`
  - **Role**: The spirit of the original *Necronomicon* (`ネクロノミコン`), Kurou's partner and lover.
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `妾` (*warawa* -> `"I"`), addresses others with haughty familiarity (`貴様`, `汝`, `九郎` -> `"Kurou"`, `"Fool"`).
  - **Voice & Register**: Imperious, archaic-tinged aristocratic pride mixed with tsundere affection for Kurou and maternal warmth toward Kuzaku. Avoid contractions in her serious declarations (`"Do not falter, Kurou!"`, `"Indeed"`, `"Very well"`, `"Insolent cur!"`), keeping her diction regal, elevated, and commanding.

- **Kuzaku (九朔 / クザク)** — `Speaker: 九朔`
  - **Role**: Mysterious girl who falls from a dimensional rift alongside a damaged Demonbane; born as the vessel connecting Kurou and Al across worlds.
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `わたし` (`"I"`). Calls Kurou `お父さま` (`"Daddy"` / `"Father"`) and Al `お母さま` (`"Mother"`).
  - **Voice & Register**: Pure, earnest, polite, and childlike in early chapters, gradually revealing profound sorrow and self-sacrificing determination as her true origin (`Two-Sword`) surfaces.

- **Two-Sword / Futatsurugi (トゥーソード / 二剣)** — `Speaker: トゥーソード`
  - **Role**: Kuzaku's true cross-dimensional combat persona (`Elder God` hunter who traverses broken worlds wielding the twin blades of Kurou and Al alongside `Demonbane Two-Sword`).
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `俺` (`"I"`).
  - **Voice & Register**: Cold, clipped, battle-hardened, and fatalistic. Speaks in terse, uncompromising sentences (`"Evil shall be purged"`, `"Become rust on my twin swords"`), masking deep grief beneath stoic ruthlessness.

- **Another Blood (アナザーブラッド)** — `Speaker: アナザーブラッド`
  - **Role**: The `girl in red` (`赤き少女`) — the sentient `Necronomicon Blood Language Edition` (`ネクロノミコン血文字版`) born from the shed blood of Kurou and Al, piloting `Demonbane Blood`.
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `私` (`"I"`). Calls Kurou `お父様` (`"Daddy"` / `"Father"`) and Al `お母様` (`"Mother"`) with bitter, venomous irony or desperate longing.
  - **Voice & Register**: Oscillates between cruel, mocking theatricality and raw, wounded existential anguish over being a "fake" (`まがい物`) unloved by her parents.

- **Hadou Ruri (覇道瑠璃 / 瑠璃)** — `Speaker: 瑠璃`
  - **Role**: Sixteen-year-old president of the `Hadou Enterprise` (`覇道財閥`) and commander of Demonbane's underground hangar.
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `私` (`"I"`). Calls Kurou `"Kurou"`, Al `"Al"`, Winfield `"Winfield"`.
  - **Voice & Register**: Refined, composed, aristocratic `ojou-sama` who speaks with crisp authority during operations, occasionally snapping in comedic frustration at Kurou's denseness or mountains of reconstruction paperwork.

- **Winfield (ウィンフィールド)** — `Speaker: ウィンフィールド`
  - **Role**: Head butler of the Hadou household and master boxer capable of fighting superhuman foes barehanded.
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `私` (`"I"`). Calls Ruri `お嬢様` (`"Mistress"`), Kurou `九郎様` (`"Master Kurou"`).
  - **Voice & Register**: Impeccably formal, courteous, composed butler diction (`"Understood, Mistress"`, `"Allow me to handle this"`), even while unleashing devastating martial arts strikes.

- **Leica Crusade (ライカ・クルセイド / ライカ)** — `Speaker: ライカ`
  - **Role**: Gentle nun at the Arkham church who cares for the orphans (`George`, `Stone`, `Colin`); formerly Laban Shrewsbury's artificial superhuman test subject (`Crusade`).
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `私` (`"I"`). Calls Kurou `"Kurou-kun"` or `"Kurou"`.
  - **Voice & Register**: Warm, gentle, nurturing, and polite; takes on a quiet, sorrowful resolve when confronting Shrewsbury and her past.

- **Laban Shrewsbury (ラバン・シュリュズベリイ / シュリュズベリイ)** & **Hazuki (ハヅキ)** — `Speaker: シュリュズベリイ` / `ハヅキ`
  - **Role**: Professor of `Miskatonic University` who fights evil through ruthless, clinical sorcery alongside `Hazuki`, the spirit of the `Celaeno Fragments` (`セラエノ断章`), piloting `iyan-hoto` / `Lord Byakhee`.
  - **Voice & Register**: Shrewsbury is cold, academic, unsentimental, and pragmatic; Hazuki is brisk, analytical, and dutifully loyal (`"Professor"`).

- **Chiaki (チアキ), Makoto (マコト), Sonia (ソニア)** — `Speaker: チアキ` / `マコト` / `ソニア`
  - **Role**: The three bridge operators of the Hadou underground base.
  - **Voice & Register**: Chiaki is bubbly, energetic, and informal; Makoto is cool, deadpan, and concise; Sonia is soft-spoken, anxious, and earnest.

### Black Lodge, Anticross & Cosmic Entities

- **Master Therion (マスターテリオン / テリオン)** — `Speaker: テリオン`
  - **Role**: Grand Master of the `Black Lodge` (`ブラックロッジ`), the `Great Beast` (`大導師`), pilot of `Liber Legis` (`リベル・レギス`).
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `僕` (`"I"`). Calls Nyarlathotep `"Teaser"` (`揶揄う者`) / `"Gamekeeper"` (`ゲームキーパー`).
  - **Voice & Register**: Detached, languid, omnipotent boredom mixed with icy aristocratic grandeur. Speaks in calm, philosophical declarations.

- **Etheldreda (エセルドレーダ)** — `Speaker: エセルドレーダ`
  - **Role**: Spirit of the `Pnakotic Manuscripts` (`ナコト写本`), Therion's devoted grimoire.
  - **Voice & Register**: Reverent, solemn, utterly devoted to Master Therion (`"My Lord"`).

- **Doctor West (ドクター・ウェスト)** — `Speaker: ドクター・ウェスト` / `ウェスト`
  - **Role**: Eccentric mad scientist of the Black Lodge and builder of the `Destroyer Robot` (`破壊ロボ`) series.
  - **Pronouns & Address**: `我輩` (`"I"`). Calls Kurou `"Occult Detective, Daijuuji Kurou!"`.
  - **Voice & Register**: Bombastic, theatrical, over-the-top mad-scientist eloquence punctuated by manic laughter (`"Fuhahaha!"`, `"Nuuooorgh!"`) and rock-and-roll showmanship.

- **Elsa (エルザ)** — `Speaker: エルザ`
  - **Role**: Doctor West's combat gynoid and "daughter."
  - **Voice & Register**: Cheerful deadpan robot cadence; appends `-robo` (`ロボ`) to the end of sentences (`"Darling is here-robo!"`) and calls Kurou `"Darling"`.

- **The Anticross (アンチクロス)**:
  - **Augustus (アウグストゥス)**: Imperious, regal leader of the Anticross (`Legacy of Gold` / `レガシー・オブ・ゴールド`); speaks like a divine Roman emperor.
  - **Tiberius (ティベリウス)**: Sadistic, cackling necromancer (`De Marigny's Clock` / `Mirror of Nitocris`); nasal, mocking, and venomous.
  - **Titus (ティトゥス)**: Bloodthirsty warrior (`Unaussprechlichen Kulten` / `Ogre`); rough, bellicose, lives only for slaughter.
  - **Claudius (クラウディウス)** & **Caligula (カリギュラ)**: Arrogant, mocking speedster (`Belzebuth`) and savage brute (`Nameless One`).
  - **Ennea (エンネア)**: Childlike, eerie prophetess (`R'lyeh Text` / `Kraken`); speaks in dreamy, detached third-person or childlike cadence.
  - **Vespasianus (ウェスパシアヌス)**: Grotesque fused monstrosity of the fallen Anticross (`Galba, Otho, Vitellius`).

- **Azrad (アズラッド)** — `Speaker: アズラッド`
  - **Role**: Al Azif's previous master from antiquity, pilot of `Aeon` (`アイオーン`), tragic husband of `Uarda` (`ウアルダ`).
  - **Voice & Register**: Solemn, archaic-tinged warrior-sage burdened by centuries of grief and fatalistic duty.

- **Metatron (メタトロン)** & **Sandalphon (サンダルフォン)** — `Speaker: メタトロン` / `サンダルフォン`
  - **Role**: Mechanical archangel of merciless justice and his dark twin of malice.
  - **Voice & Register**: Cold, fanatical, liturgical judgments.

- **Nyarlathotep (ナイアルラトホテップ) / Alone (アローン)** — `Speaker: ナイアルラトホテップ` / `アローン`
  - **Role**: The `Crawling Chaos` (`這い寄る混沌`), `Clockwork Phantom` (`クロックワーク・ファントム`), and `Gamekeeper` (`ゲームキーパー`) who orchestrates the eternal loop of worlds.
  - **Voice & Register**: Polished, theatrical, mocking, and chillingly omniscient; delights in observing human struggle as a stage play.
