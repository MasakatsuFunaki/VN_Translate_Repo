# 櫻の詩 — Route Guide

Seven endings and two side branches, in three playthroughs and three saves. Every option is
quoted in this project's English.

## What you collect

| Ending | Where |
|--------|-------|
| III — Pica Pica, Toritani Makoto | Save 1, first option |
| III — Olympia, Misakura Rin | Save 1, second option |
| III — Zypressen, Hikawa Rina | Save 2, first option |
| III — Märchen, Kawachino Yumi | Save 2, second option |
| III — A Nice Derangement of Epitaphs, Natsume Shizuku | playthrough 3 |
| IV — What Is Mind? No Matter. What Is Matter? Never Mind. | chapter IV |
| V — The Happy Prince and Other Tales | Save 3, first option |
| V — Natsume Ai | Save 3, second option |
| VI — the true ending | chapter VI |

Chapters IV, V and VI are separate entries on the title screen. Each one opens after the one
before it.

## Chapters IV, V and VI

Start each one from the title screen, in order.

**Chapter IV** has no choices.

**Chapter V** has two. At the first, take *Well, at your skirt length your panties won't show,
so*. It opens extra scenes, Ai's included, and does not change the ending.

**Save 3** at the second choice.

| Pick | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| I don't know. | The Happy Prince ending. Chapter VI opens. |
| Ah, I was wrong. | The Ai branch. The chapter stops there. |

> **WARNING:** Take *Ah, I was wrong* first. That branch reaches no ending and does not open
> chapter VI. Reload Save 3 and take *I don't know* to continue.

**Chapter VI** has no choices. It ends on the true ending.
